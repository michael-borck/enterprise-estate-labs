# Ticket: customers see a certificate warning

**From:** Customer Support, CloudCore Networks
**Priority:** high
**Service:** customer web portal

Customers reaching the portal see a browser warning before the page loads. Some abandon
the page. The site works if they click through, but support cannot tell them to ignore a
security warning.

## What we know

- The portal is behind a reverse proxy.
- The proxy terminates TLS.
- The certificate is generated by the proxy, not issued by a public certificate
  authority.
- The web tier itself speaks plain HTTP inside the container network.

## What we need

1. Confirm where TLS terminates and where the request is re-originated.
2. Explain why the browser warns, in language a support agent can use.
3. Decide what to do: trust the internal certificate for staff, replace it with a
   publicly trusted certificate, or change the architecture.

## Evidence we expect

- The certificate subject, issuer and expiry.
- The request path from browser to proxy to web.
- A log line that proves which service handled the request.
- A recommendation with a cost and risk note.
