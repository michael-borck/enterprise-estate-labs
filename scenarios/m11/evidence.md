# Evidence prompts

Capture the following for your portfolio:

- The exposed `config.txt` content, and the URL that served it.
- The security log lines that show the failed logins and the successful admin reads.
- A timeline with timestamps, source address, action and result.
- The secret file's mode and content before and after the fix.
- The `check-m11` output before and after the fix.
- The exact containment steps, in order, and why you chose that order.
- What you rotated, and what still needs rotation outside the container.
- The incident record: detection, scope, containment, eradication, recovery, notification,
  lessons learned and prevention.
- One place where AI was wrong, vague or over-confident about the incident.
