# Incident record

**Incident:** exposed admin configuration and database credential
**Owner:** (name)
**Severity:** (critical, high, medium, low)
**Status:** (open, contained, closed)

## 1. Detection

- Who or what reported the incident?
- When was it detected?
- What was the first evidence?

## 2. Scope

- Which systems and accounts were involved?
- What did the source do, in order?
- What else might it have reached?
- What evidence supports the scope, and what is still unknown?

## 3. Containment

- What did you change to stop the exposure?
- Why did you choose that order?
- What did you leave alone, and why?

## 4. Eradication and recovery

- What did you remove or rotate?
- Where else was the credential used?
- How did you verify the old value no longer works?
- Did the service stay available? If not, for how long?

## 5. Notification

- Who needs to know?
- What is the legal or contractual obligation?
- What did you tell customers and staff?

## 6. Lessons learned

- What would have caught this sooner?
- Which control failed: access, logging, review, secret management or something else?
- What will you change, who owns it and when?

## 7. Evidence retained

- Logs, screenshots, commands and outputs.
- Where the evidence is stored and who can access it.
